PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 27, 2026. CREVINOX ("we", "us", or "our") respects the intellectual privacy and confidentiality of visitors and prospective clients. This policy clarifies our data protection standards.

1. Information We Collect

When you initiate a project inquiry via our contact form or direct communication channels, we collect the details you voluntarily provide: your name, email address, organization or business name, project scope, budget range, and project brief.

2. Purpose of Processing

We process inquiry details solely to evaluate project fit, formulate architectural proposals, and communicate regarding web development consultations. We do not sell, rent, or trade client information to any third parties under any circumstances.

3. Telemetry & Analytics

We may use privacy-conscious analytics (such as Google Analytics 4) to monitor aggregated website metrics (e.g., page views, general geographic region, and device categories) to optimize loading speed and accessibility. IP addresses are anonymized where applicable.

4. Direct WhatsApp Communication

If you choose to initiate a consultation via WhatsApp, interactions are governed by WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption protocols and privacy policies.

5. Contacting the Studio

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request the deletion of any inquiry correspondence, contact us at: crewinox@gmail.com.
